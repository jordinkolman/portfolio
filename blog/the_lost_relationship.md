# The Lost Relationship Between Work and Identity

In medieval systems, labor was often tied deeply to community survival and the rhythms of nature, rather than an abstracted metric of productivity. 

## The Abstraction of Identity

Modern systems emphasize the individual as a unit of output. We can learn a lot from the historical transition from agrarian serfdom to industrial and post-industrial mechanics.

Here are some points to consider:
1. Meaning was localized.
2. Rhythms dictated labor, rather than labor dictating rhythms.
3. The "Legacy" was the community, not the individual.
